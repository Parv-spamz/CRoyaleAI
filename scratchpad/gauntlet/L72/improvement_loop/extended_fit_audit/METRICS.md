seven views in PLAN, all eight card strata, all/PLAY/Rocket/late Rocket. Counts
use original expert labels and legal card choices from cached logits and public
elixir/hand masks. Continuous distance decodes lattice cell x/36,y/64 and scales
by18,32tiles; <=1 is literal, without epsilon. Gate is >.35 and at least one legal
card. Full PLAY requires gate+card+aim. Correct WAIT requires no called PLAY.

multiplicity from all1024000draws,510080native/513920mirror. Never-drawn means no

distinct replay denominators must union IDs, never sum overlapping replays.
