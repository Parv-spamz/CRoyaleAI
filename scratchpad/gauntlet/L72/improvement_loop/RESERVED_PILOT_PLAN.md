# First reserved native reconstruction pilot

This is data qualification, not successor training, model selection or final
confirmation inference. Preserve the original immutable reservation. After the
twenty-replay local preflight passes, collect one bounded serial CPU batch:

- All11 native-compatible exact-Icebow confirmation groups.
- For each existing split, the first32 groups in each of the seven metadata
  strata (Rocket, Rocket/Tornado, X-Bow, Log/Barrel, Witch, Night Witch, Furnace)
  ordered by SHA256(`clashbot-l72-native-pilot-v1:` + command signature).
- The first32 groups per split under the same ordering without a stratum filter,
  to retain an ordinary-play qualification sample. Deduplicate the union.

No outcomes, model predictions, success labels or deck substitutions enter
selection. General-deck examples remain explicitly general-deck. They do not
replace missing exact-Icebow spawner/rare-sequence confirmation. This pilot is
for usable-data yield and denominator discovery;32 is not a power calculation.
More untouched evidence and the full statistical design remain required by N2.

Verify raw part hashes, reserved membership/signatures and original forms. Use
the reviewed hf_to_crawl conversion, then independently compare every generated
command's tick/side/orientation/card/ability/coordinates against its raw event.
Bind raw payload, CSV and membership hashes. No aliases or form stripping.

Only after native_capture_v2_verified.json and its successful process receipt
validate, record every10 ticks with full native/public fields and driven
abilities, seed424242, level11, slack40, tail7200. Repeat every25th selected group
with the same settings. A usable recording must have all source commands driven
and accepted, no skipped/invalid commands or elixir delays, matching crowns,
terminal outcome, valid public fields and successful scheduled determinism.
Preserve all rejected attempts. Report yields by split, original stratum, deck
and failure reason. Never promote a failed/partial reconstruction to usable.

Stop on service/protocol/source drift or repeat nondeterminism. Ordinary replay
incompatibilities are recorded as exclusions and do not reassign or replace
groups. No automatic retry or adaptive sampling until a separately documented
collection plan. Outputs stay in ignored icebow/data; source and summary receipts
are published. Neither this batch nor passing capture checks authorizes training.
