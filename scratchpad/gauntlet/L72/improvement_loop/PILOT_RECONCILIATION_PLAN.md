# Independent reserved-pilot reconciliation

This read-only check follows the existing fixed645 collection. It neither calls
the native service nor edits its sources or outputs. It must wait for the final
successful collection receipt before reporting full reconciliation.

Recompute outcome-blind selection from the immutable compatible groups and salt;
check reservation split/signature/deck membership, all source and CSV hashes,
the645 ordered jobs and26 scheduled repeats. Recount driven/accepted/skipped
commands, placements, delays and crown agreement from raw recorded command logs
and source CSVs, without calling the collector's qualification function. Verify
command indices, source ticks, sides and rotated coordinates. Compare the new
grade and exclusion decisions with every saved summary. Recheck public fields,
and independently compare repeat lifetimes and all non-ID fields. A mismatched
receipt, source, membership, recording or count fails reconciliation.

Preserve source-incompatible exclusions and missing scheduled repeats explicitly;
a replay rejected before capture cannot supply a deterministic repeat. Report
usable counts by original split/stratum/deck and source-command coverage. These
are reconstruction denominators, not opportunity counts, power or model quality.
No model is loaded, and confirmation is inspected only for fixed data-quality
criteria. No new training or final confirmation inference is authorized here.

Before trusting the check, use a positive synthetic recording and deliberately
corrupt its membership, count, acceptance, delay, crown, target and repeat data.
An optional ten-training-record smoke checks integration while collection runs;
it must not emit the full-completion token. Preserve receipts for failures.
