# Correct the training dependency after the owner's coverage challenge

October 5, following the owner's request to recheck replay coverage and explain
why fresh live data was blocking successor work. This is Codex's proposed workflow
correction, implemented under the standing authorization to proceed with
evidence-based proposals. It is not a claim that the owner waived deployment
criteria, that a new model exists, or that live bot actions are expert labels.

The prior dependency made complete final-confirmation coverage a prerequisite
for every successor optimization. That is stronger than needed for controlled
development. The corrected dependency is:

1. Before development optimization, freeze allowed training/development replay
   groups, their hashes and exposure, original labels, the one-factor recipe,
   fixed update budget, controls and development selection rule. Do not use the
   inspected L71 validation for tuning, any reserved pro confirmation replay, or
   the owner's live actions as expert training targets.
2. Development can use existing expert split0 data. A deterministic replay-level
   inner development split is excluded from new gradient updates. R1e already
   encountered those replays during parent training: this is disclosed, and the
   resulting measurements are development evidence, never fresh generalization.
3. Complete adequate untouched confirmation, physical outcome qualification,
   statistical power/multiplicity and matched gameplay remain mandatory before
   final confirmation testing, acceptance or autonomous deployment. Preserve
   every numerical replacement floor in PLAN.md. Historical regression results
   and the new source-only low-HP inventory cannot satisfy those gates alone.

This supersedes only the blanket 'all N2 complete before any successor training'
dependency in earlier continuation text. N2 as a final-evidence gate remains
open. Data isolation and a frozen development experiment are still required
before launch. No experiment is automatically authorized to consume confirmation
data, adapt a test threshold or ship a failed model.

Immediate work is development_iteration_1: ordinary corrected-body IL versus the
same IL with only the generic learned projectile-target residual, both from R1e.
This removes the harmful Rocket/X-Bow mixture confound observed in the original
experiments. A separate later sequence curriculum can use the verified expert
defense index and the owner's broader cycle contexts with original PLAY/WAIT/card/
aim labels retained. It must not fabricate 'cast Rocket' targets from tower HP.

The owner's broader finishing definition calls for potential one-to-three-Rocket
cycle windows, not just successful expert final blows. coverage_recheck v2 proves
many low-HP states exist. Exact shot counts still need level/damage provenance;
available elixir, rotation, time and defensive punishment determine whether a
cycle is feasible. New labels/metrics need training/development validation before
any final policy test; no confirmation-driven threshold tuning.
