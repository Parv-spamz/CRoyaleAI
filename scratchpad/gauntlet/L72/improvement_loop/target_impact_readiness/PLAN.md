# Controlled spell-impact instrument before learned targeting

Registered October5 21:12 EDT after outcome_lambda1_v5 completed and failed its
unchanged continuation floors. The owner asked for a methodology change: test
whether a mechanism can learn and measure physical consequences before another
broad successor run. This first leaf qualifies a measurement instrument only.

Existing expert aim-within-one-tile labels are coordinate agreement, not impact.
No previous failed verdict is changed. No new model, policy predictions, training,
expert labels, confirmation access, native replay collection or live change.

Fixed CPU-only updated RoyaleSim/RoyaleGym fixtures: sixteen roots comprising
both firing sides, both enemy princess lanes, root ticks90/4800 and card/tower
levels11/14. Both decks are a fixed base-form Icebow deck reordered with Rocket
first for the synthetic fixture. All units/forms/levels and tower identity are
recorded; this is not a source replay or representative tactical distribution.
Reset at tick0 and advance naturally to the root; no HP/elixir manipulation.

Each root has seven branches from the exact same serialized engine state:
WAIT, exact repeated WAIT, Rocket at target center, at inward horizontal offsets
1/2/3 tiles, and at6 tiles toward arena midline vertically. Commands land after
26 ticks; run300 ticks beyond landing, recording every tick. Opponents and our
side issue no additional cards/abilities in these isolated scenes. No policy is
made to choose these fixture commands. The fixed center/far cases are assay
controls, not tactical rules, targets for expert imitation, or spell rewards.

Save original root/final engine bytes plus compact native frames (all six crown
entities with identities/positions/HP/maxHP/footprints, both player elixir/crowns,
tick/outcome, spell/projectile counts). Demand six complete unique crown slots
and no troops/buildings in every frame. Measured effect is WAIT HP minus cast HP
at the same tick for each tower. Charge actual accepted command cost and retain
both-lane/king effects. Record exact first damage tick and plateau damage.
No radius threshold is inferred before measurement and no impact is inferred
from proximity. No claims about safe cycles, moving targets or useful gameplay.

R1: collect the112 branches once with runtime/source binding and exact duplicate
WAIT byte/trace agreement. R2: a separate verifier imports no producer or engine,
recomputes all per-tower effects from raw frames, checks full membership/ticks,
command/cost accounting, source hashes and corruption controls. R3: closeout
binds both receipts/results and scopes what a future mechanism may use.

Required controls: unchanged WAIT (zero damage), center (positive target-only
damage), far (zero crown damage), exact duplicated WAIT; corrupted identity,
missing frame/tower, HP/effect, timeline, cost, command and branch membership must
fail the independent verifier. Offset effects are descriptive and never used to
change pass thresholds. Assay outcomes qualify only this pinned simulator and
stationary isolated crown scenes, not native-client parity or policy acceptance.

Only source files inside this new leaf plus the reviewed runtime/pipeline loader
and this PLAN/METRICS are bound. Frozen prior experiments remain untouched.
No model report is due. Later targeting learning, moving units/multiple targets,
public-only action scoring, real-client parity and matched gameplay require
