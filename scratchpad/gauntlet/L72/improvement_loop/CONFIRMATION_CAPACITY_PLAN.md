# Capacity check before successor experiment design

Join only the two independently verified native collections. Keep every replay's
original split, signature, forms and source hashes. Exclude all failed records;
prove the collections have no overlapping tags/signatures. Do not recollect or
change availability criteria. The result is an inventory, not a trainable dataset.

For qualified exact-Icebow sides only, count accepted original card commands,
including opposing Goblin Barrels and each spawner family. This establishes
simple upper bounds on available component events before implementing detailed
opportunity labels or powering a comparison. A Barrel cast is not necessarily
an unambiguous preemptive-Log opportunity; repeated casts/replay sides are not
independent replays. A Rocket cast is not automatically a finish/cycle/combo.

Write a compact per-replay manifest bound to the independent reports/recordings
and aggregate counts. Check its totals independently against the immutable raw
CSV commands and qualified source-deck metadata. No model predictions, tuning,
optimization, outcome-based sampling, form inference or thresholds change.

If required evidence remains absent/too sparse, N2 stays open. Record the precise
shortfall and continue faithful data acquisition/preparation; other-deck examples
cannot silently replace missing exact-Icebow evidence.
